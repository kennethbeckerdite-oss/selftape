Reader direction tags

Type these into a reader line: [pause] [pause 2] [beat] [slow] [fast] [soft] [loud] [high] [low] [normal]. A tag changes everything after it in that line. Screenplay parentheticals aren't spoken; (beat) becomes a 1-second pause. Free voices can't sigh or laugh, so tags like [sighs] are skipped for now.
